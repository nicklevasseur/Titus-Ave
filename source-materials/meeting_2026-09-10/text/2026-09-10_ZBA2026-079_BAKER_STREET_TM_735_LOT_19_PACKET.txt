Zoning Relief Required for Use Permit

Your application for a permit dated August 14, 2026 to establish a seasonal use of the sale of Christmas

trees on the lot for a duration of 45 days where 30 days is allowed, with an office trailer on the lot

located 1’ from the front lot line where 10’ is required, and install a 6’ high temporary fence in the

front yard where 4’ is allowed in the MX-1 Zoning District, all as per documents submitted through

August 14, 2026 and with non-compliance listed in the Zoning Review dated August 26, 2026 is hereby

acknowledged.

According to Section(s) 4.7.4 Seasonal Sales of Goods Not Produced on the Lot, 4.7.1.A.1 Duration of

Seasonal Sales, 4.7.4.A.3 Accessory Structures Associated with Seasonal Sales, and 8.2.4-A Fence

Height — Front Yard of the Zoning Ordinance of the City of Manchester, New Hampshire, passed to be

ordained on March 1, 2026, this is prohibited. Consequently, | have no authority to grant this permit.

Permit is hereby DENIED.

Further proceedings contemplated pertaining to this application must be pursuant to NH Revised

Statutes Annotated 674:33 or the other statutory provisions relative to Zoning Boards of Adjustment,

as may be appropriate.

On behalf of Scouting America Troop 106B and 106G (collectively, “Troop 106”),

as we plan our Christmas Tree Sales Fundraiser for the holiday season.

fund a full calendar year of Scouting activities for the boys and girls of Troop 106.
Your support plays a meaningful role in sustaining a program that teaches
leadership, service, and character to youth in our community.
As we prepare for the Christmas season, we would like to outline our planned
schedule, operations, and commitments for your review.
Operating Dates and Site Use
We respectfully request the use of the space from one week before Thanksgiving
through one week after Christmas, specifically November 19 through December
31.
e Nov 19-Nov 27: Site setup, fencing, lighting installation, and delivery of
trees. ;
e Sales Period: From the Friday after Thanksgiving through just before
Christmas.
e Dec 26—Dec 31: Breakdown, cleanup, and full restoration of the site. ;
Sales Hours (Weather Permitting)
e Monday—Friday: 4:00 PM to 8:00 or 9:00 PM
e Saturday—Sunday: 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM

e Supplying our own power, porta-potty, and office trailer
e Installing perimeter fencing to clearly define the operating area
e Providing lighting and security cameras for safety during and after selling
hours :
e Maintaining 1-2cars on site per shift, with brief periods of 3-4cars during
. shift changes j
e Ensuring that trained adult leaders are present at all times to supervise
youth whenever they are on site
e Managing our own trash removal, including supplying our own barrels and
removing full bags regularly throughout the six-week period

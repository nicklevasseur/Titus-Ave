Variance Criteria Villiard 50 Delia Drive July, 2026
Sections of the Zoni i di ief:
The existing garage/ADU is existing non-conforming with respect to being in a street yard.

1. The variance would not be contrary to the public interest because:

Granting variance would not be contrary to the public interest because it will not alter the essential character of
the neighborhood. The purpose of the addition of two rooms to the existing garage/ADU structure is to provide
diverse affordable housing without the need for additional infrastructure or land development. The existing
garage/ADU was constructed before the applicant purchased the property. The applicant purchased the
property in 2003. The addition will be the same height and width of the existing structure. This addition will not
affect the distance of the current structure to the two neighbors on this dead end street. The addition will
extend 16 feet from the existing structure towards the road. This would leave 57 feet of land from the structure
to the road.

2. By granting the variance, the use would not be contrary to the Spirit of the Zoning Ordinance because:
The intent of the zoning ordinance is to promote the efficient development of the property, prevent
overcrowding and protect the health, safety and welfare of the public. The applicant is proposing an addition of
two rooms with a garage below for support. The proposed addition will not encroach further than the existing
structure on either side of the property. The addition will be the same height and width of the existing structure.
The requested variance would not threaten the public health, safety, or welfare, or overcrowd the
neighborhood, as the lot already existed with a garage/ADU on a lot with 0.67 acres in a residential area. It is
our hope that the ZBA would agree this request is reasonable and within the spirit of the ordinance and should
be approved.

3. By granting the variance, substantial justice would be done because:

Approval of the variance would allow the applicant to add two rooms to an existing one bedroom ADU with one
combined living space. This would allow a family to have a separate bedroom and play area for a child. We
can see no benefit to the public by denying this variance request. Should the application be denied, the loss to
the applicant would be substantial. Therefore, as the loss to the applicant would greatly outweigh any
perceived public gain, it is our opinion that this variance should be approved.

4. The proposed use would not diminish surrounding property values because:

The value of the existing garage/ADU would increase the value and function of the property by adding two
rooms to an existing two room dwelling. Approval of this variance request will not reduce the value of
Surrounding properties.

5. Literal enforcement of the provisions of the ordinance would result in unnecessary hardship if owing to
the following special conditions of the property that distinguish it from other properties in the area
because:

No fair and substantial relationship exists between the general purposes of the ordinance provisions and the
specific application of that provision to the property. Also, the proposed use is a reasonable one. The proposed
use is a residential addition to an existing residence in a residential area.

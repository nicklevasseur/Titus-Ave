RE: ZBA2026-076, 801 Hanover Street, B-4 :
Zoning Relief Required for Use Permit
Your application for a permit dated August 7, 2026 to establish a professional chiropractor office use,
on a lot with 10,760 SF of buildable lot area where 15,000 SF is required, with parking in the front
yard, where the required 5’ landscape buffer is not maintained around the perimeter of the parking
area, with wall signage area of 7.7% of the primary facade where 3% is allowed, and where the wall
signs total 40.9 SF where 32 SF is allowed in the R-1B Zoning District, all as per documents submitted
through August 8, 2026 and with non-compliance listed in the Zoning Review dated August 17, 2026 is
hereby acknowledged.
According to Section(s) 4.3.1-A Medical Clinic, 6.2-B.G Minimum Buildable Lot Area, 6.2-B-I Surface
Parking Location, 8.1.3 Parking Lot Perimeter Landscape, and 9.6-A Allowable Signage (2 counts) of
the Zoning Ordinance of the City of Manchester, New Hampshire, passed to be ordained on March 1,
2026, this is prohibited. Consequently, | have no authority to grant this permit. Permit is hereby
DENIED.

Zoning Relief Required for Building Permit
Your application for a permit dated August 12, 2026 to maintain the expansion of a parking area to
create two new parking spaces in the front yard, located directly in front of the primary facade, that
require backing out onto the sidewalk to exit through the curb cut, where the driveway width is 50’
at the property line where 24’ is allowed, where the two spaces are within 2’ of the building and one
space is undersized at 15’ 5” where 18.5’ is required, with lot coverage of 99% where 90% is allowed,
and without the required Type | Landscape Buffer around the parking area in the R-1B Zoning District,
all as per documents submitted through August 12, 2026 and with non-compliance listed in the Zoning
Review dated August 20, 2026 is hereby acknowledged.
According to Section(s) 5.3.1.F.3.1 Surface Parking Location, 7.5.2.B.2 Principal Frontage Parking,
7.5.2.D Safety of Parking Design, 7.4.2.A Maximum Driveway Width, 7.5.2.4 Parking Setbacks (2
counts), 7.3-A.B Parking Layout, 5.3.1.F.3.H Maximum Lot Coverage, and 8.1.1.B Type I Landscape
Buffer of the Zoning Ordinance of the City of Manchester, New Hampshire, passed to be ordained on
March 1, 2026, this is prohibited. Consequently, | have no authority to grant this permit. Permit is
hereby DENIED.
Further proceedings contemplated pertaining to this application must be pursuant to NH Revised
Statutes Annotated 674:33 or the other statutory provisions relative to Zoning Boards of Adjustment,
as may be appropriate.

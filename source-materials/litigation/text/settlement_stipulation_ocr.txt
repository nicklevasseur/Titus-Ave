SETTLEMENT STIPULATION
NOW COME the Plaintiff, T & L 2018, LLC, and the Defendants, Nam Van Tran, Thao
Phuong Tran, Daniel Allard, Jennifer Allard, Jacob Hall, Rasheed A. Chinn, Benito J. Cecenas,
Tracie J. Cecenas, The Helen B. Horne 2006 Revocable Trust, Katharine-Lee M. Larochelle,

as follows:
BACKGROUND
1. A Complaint for Declaratory Judgment to Quiet Title was filed with this Court on
November 4, 2025 by the Petitioner, T & L 2018, LLC.
2. On December 26, 2025 the Court issued a Notice of Default against Defendant, Mount
Zion Christian Schools, Inc.
3. Each of the remaining named Defendants, specifically, Nam Van Tran, Thao Phuong
Tran, Daniel Allard, Jennifer Allard, Jacob Hall, Rasheed A. Chinn, Benito J. Cecenas,
Tracie J. Cecenas, The Helen B. Horne 2006 Revocable Trust, Katharine-Lee M.
Larochelle, Timothy J. Larochelle, and Kennedy Family Revocable Trust of 2014, and
the City of Manchester, NH have entered appearances in this matter.
4. Pursuant to the Report of the Guardian Ad Litem filed with the Court, no other parties
have a potential interest in the property at issue known as Bourne Street in Manchester,
New Hampshire (the “Property”).

5. Each of the remaining parties in this matter have reached an agreement as to the
disposition of the parcel of land known as Bourne Street (a/k/a Bourne Avenue, a/k/a
Beech Avenue) in Manchester, New Hampshire as shown on Hillsborough County
Registry of Deeds Plan #16103 and as more precisely depicted in the proposed plan
entitled “Bourne Street Disposition Plan” Map 554 Lot 17C, prepared by The Dubay
Group, Inc. (the “Property”).

AGREEMENT

6. The remaining parties agree that the Property shall be divided among the remaining
parties as set forth in the attached Schedules A and B.

7. Plaintiff, T & L 2018, LLC and the Defendants, Nam Van Tran, Thao Phuong Tran,
Daniel Allard, Jennifer Allard, Jacob Hall, Rasheed A. Chinn, Benito J. Cecenas, Tracie
J. Cecenas, The Helen B. Horne 2006 Revocable Trust, Katharine-Lee M. Larochelle,
Timothy J. Larochelle, and Kennedy Family Revocable Trust of 2014, and the City of
Manchester, NH have submitted the attached proposed Order which, upon execution by
the Court, shall be recorded with the Hillsborough County Registry of Deeds.
